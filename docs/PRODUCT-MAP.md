# QADHA — Product Map

## Connected product objects

| Object | Role in the product |
|---|---|
| Viewer / Creator | Content discovery, viewing and publishing |
| Army | Persistent team identity, membership and ranks |
| Battle | Structured competition with server-owned phases and results |
| Wallet | Test balances and virtual-item catalogues |
| Operations Console | Content, moderation, catalog, rules and audit workflows |

## Operating console

The internal console covers:

- Video upload, publishing, featuring and removal.
- Moderation queues, sanctions and appeals.
- Gift and catalog configuration.
- Battle tools and versioned rulesets.
- Role management and audited sensitive changes.
- Authorized content migration with rights checks and de-duplication.

The console is internal. This public document does not expose its routes or access details.
