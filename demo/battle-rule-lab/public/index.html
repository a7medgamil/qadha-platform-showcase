    02 / WHY THIS TECHNICAL DECISION

    Small scope. Deliberate tradeoffs.

    Built for inspection
    01

    One owner of state

    Proposals carry an expected revision. A pure engine validates the actor, state and time gate before applying a complete change.

    Tradeoff Stale clients must refresh and submit a new key. A real service also needs authenticated actors and atomic durable storage.

    02

    A reason for every decision

    Accepts, rejections and exact retries enter an ordered audit. A reused key with a changed payload is a conflict.

    Tradeoff The trail grows in memory and disappears on refresh. Production retention, tamper resistance and capacity remain unproven.

    03

    Honest feedback over optimism

    The interface shows the engine’s decision before reflecting a change. The guided replay exposes failures as part of the story.

    Tradeoff Explicit rejection adds friction. Real network latency, accessibility beyond this review and competitive fairness need separate validation.
